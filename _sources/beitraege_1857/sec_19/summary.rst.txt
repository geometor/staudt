Summary: § 19. The Concept and Addition of Throws (arts. 256–267)
=================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Beiträge, Zweites Heft* (1857), § 19 (arts. 256–267)

* **Throw (*Wurf*, § 19 art. 256)**: An ordered projective equivalence class :math:`(ABCD)` of four collinear elements; fixing gauge triad :math:`(P_0, P_1, P_\infty)` identifies each point :math:`X \in \ell \setminus \{P_\infty\}` with :math:`(P_\infty P_0 P_1 X)`.
* **Straightedge Addition (§ 19 art. 259)**: Centers :math:`U, V` collinear with :math:`P_\infty` and axis :math:`a = [P_\infty R]` (:math:`R = [U P_0] \cap [V X]`) construct :math:`S = X + Y` by a parabolic double perspectivity fixing :math:`P_\infty`.

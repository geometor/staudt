Summary: § 19. The Concept and Addition of Throws (arts. 256–267)
=================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Beiträge, Zweites Heft* (1857), § 19 (arts. 256–267)

* **Throw (*Wurf*, § 19 art. 256)**: An ordered projective equivalence class $(ABCD)$ of four collinear elements; fixing gauge triad $(P_0, P_1, P_\infty)$ identifies each point $X \in \ell \setminus \{P_\infty\}$ with $(P_\infty P_0 P_1 X)$.
* **Straightedge Addition (§ 19 art. 259)**: Centers $U, V$ collinear with $P_\infty$ and axis $a = [P_\infty R]$ ($R = [U P_0] \cap [V X]$) construct $S = X + Y$ by a parabolic double perspectivity fixing $P_\infty$.

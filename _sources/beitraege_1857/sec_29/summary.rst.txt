Summary: § 29. The Harmonic Scale & Projective Abscissae (arts. 403–404)
========================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Beiträge, Zweites Heft* (1857), § 29 (arts. 403–404)

* **Harmonic Scale & Projective Abscissae (§ 29 arts. 403–404)**: Iterated sums and quotients construct every rational coordinate $P_{p/q}$ by straightedge alone, coordinatizing $\mathbb{P}^1$ intrinsically without metric distance.

Critical Notes: § 1. Space, Surfaces, Lines, and Points (arts. 1–15)
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Scholarly Commentary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 1 (arts. 1–15)

* **Spatial Incidence**: In § 1 (arts. 1–15), von Staudt lays down the basic spatial incidence foundations without coordinates: points, lines, planes, and their intersections. In modern synthetic terms (compare :person:`Hilbert <hilbert>` Group I and :person:`Pasch <pasch>` Grundsätze I–VIII), these establish the fundamental connection axioms governing incidence in three dimensions.

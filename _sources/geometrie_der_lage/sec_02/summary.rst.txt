Summary: § 2. Parallelism and Infinitely Distant Elements (arts. 16–22)
=======================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 2 (arts. 16–22)

* **Adjoining Ideal Elements via Parallel Sheaves (§ 2)**: Each parallel direction (*Richtung* / *Parallelstrahlenbündel*) defines an :term:`ideal point <unendlich-fernes-element>` :math:`P_\infty`; coplanar directions form an ideal line :math:`g_\infty`, and all directions in space form the single ideal plane :math:`\omega_\infty`, eliminating all parallel exceptions.

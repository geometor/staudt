Critical Notes: § 3. The Law of Reciprocity (arts. 23–35)
=========================================================

.. rubric:: Scholarly Commentary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 3 (arts. 23–35)

* **The Principle of Duality**: Von Staudt's systematic formulation of the *Gesetz der Reciprocität* in § 3 (Art. 25) unifies Poncelet and Gergonne's earlier notions into an intrinsic structural property of completed projective space. Every theorem in 3-space possesses an immediate dual twin obtained by interchanging points and planes.

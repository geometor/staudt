§ 4. Cyclical Order and Mutual Separation (arts. 36–44)
=======================================================

.. rubric:: Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 4 (arts. 36–44)

**Art. 36 (§ 4).** Every closed projective straight line is a single returning cycle (*geschlossene Linie*): a single point :math:`A` does not divide the projective line into two severed halves, because one may pass from one side of :math:`A` to the other through :math:`P_\infty`. Two distinct points :math:`A` and :math:`B` divide the entire projective line into two complementary segments; two pairs :math:`(A, B)` and :math:`(C, D)` are :term:`mutually separating <getrennte-paare>` when each segment bounded by :math:`A, B` contains one point of :math:`(C, D)`.

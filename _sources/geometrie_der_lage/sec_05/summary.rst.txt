Summary: § 5. Uniform Fundamental Forms and Perspectivity (arts. 45–53)
=======================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 5 (arts. 45–53)

* **Uniform Fundamental Forms (§ 5)**: Defines the three projectively equivalent 1D carriers—point-range (*gerades Gebilde*), flat ray-pencil (*ebenes Strahlbüschel*), and axial plane-pencil (*Ebenenbüschel*)—connected by central projection and planar section (*perspektivische Lage*).

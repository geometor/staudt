Critical Notes: § 9. Involutions in Uniform Fundamental Forms (arts. 103–115)
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Scholarly Commentary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 9 (arts. 103–115)

* **Bridge to *Beiträge* Heft 1 (1856) — Synthetic Imaginary Points**: Notice how § 9 (Art. 109) and § 19 (Art. 245) prepare von Staudt's next triumph (:cite:`Beiträge, Heft 1, 1856 <staudt:1856>`): when an exterior line :math:`g` fails to meet :math:`\mathcal{C}` in real points, :math:`\pi` **still** induces a completely well-defined real elliptic involution on :math:`g`! Equipping that elliptic involution with one of its two opposite cyclical senses :math:`(A B A' B')` vs. :math:`(A B' A' B)` gave geometry its first rigorous, purely real synthetic model of **conjugate complex intersections** without :math:`\sqrt{-1}`.

Summary: § 9. Involutions in Uniform Fundamental Forms (arts. 103–115)
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 9 (arts. 103–115)

* **Single-Swap Criterion for Involution (§ 9, art. 103)**: Any projectivity on a 1D carrier that swaps a single distinct pair :math:`A \leftrightarrow A'` swaps **every** point with its image (:math:`\tau^2 = \mathrm{id}`).
* **Hyperbolic vs. Elliptic Involutions (§ 9, art. 109)**: Non-separating pairs determine two real double points dividing every conjugate pair harmonically; separating pairs yield an elliptic involution without real double points.

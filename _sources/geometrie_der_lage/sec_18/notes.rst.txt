Critical Notes: § 18. Planar Polar Systems (arts. 235–241)
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Scholarly Commentary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 18 (arts. 235–241)

* **Planar Polar Systems Across GEOMETOR**: Compare ``geometor/altshiller-court`` (Chapters IX–X on poles and polars of the circle) and ``geometor/pappus`` on dual harmonic configurations. Von Staudt establishes polar systems purely from incidence and involution, before any conic is defined.

Unit 3 Summary: Involutions, Polarities & Conics Without Cones
==============================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Synopsis of *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), §§ 9 & 18–19

1. **Single-Swap Criterion for Involution (§ 9, art. 103)**: Any projectivity on a 1D carrier that swaps a single distinct pair $A \leftrightarrow A'$ swaps **every** point with its image ($\tau^2 = \mathrm{id}$).
2. **Hyperbolic vs. Elliptic Involutions (§ 9, art. 109)**: Non-separating pairs determine two real double points dividing every conjugate pair harmonically; separating pairs yield an elliptic involution without real double points.
3. **Planar Polar System (§ 18, arts. 235–238)**: An involutory point-line correlation $\pi$ ($Q \in \pi(P) \iff P \in \pi(Q)$) is determined by a self-polar triangle plus one generic pole–polar pair.
4. **Intrinsic Conic Definition (§ 19, arts. 242–245)**: Defines an :term:`order curve <ordnungscurve>` (conic) purely in the plane as $\{X : X \in \pi(X)\}$, instantly unifying tangents, harmonic pole-polar secants, :person:`Steiner <steiner>`'s pencil generation, and :person:`Pascal <pascal>`–:person:`Brianchon <brianchon>` duality.

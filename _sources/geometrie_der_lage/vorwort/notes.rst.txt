Critical Notes: Preface (*Vorwort*, Nuremberg, August 1847)
===========================================================

.. rubric:: Scholarly Commentary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), *Vorwort* (pp. iii–iv)

* **Straightedge-Only Complexity (``S = R``)**: By forbidding compass circles from the outset (*Vorwort*), :person:`von Staudt <staudt>` restricts every construction to pure straightedge incidences (`assert_straightedge_only(model)` in ``geometor.staudt.projective``). In Émile Lemoine's *Geometrography* (``geometor/lemoine``), every operation in ``geometor.staudt`` belongs exclusively to straightedge primitives $R_1$ (placing the ruler through a point) and $R_2$ (drawing the straight line), with compass count $C_1 = C_2 = C_3 = 0$.

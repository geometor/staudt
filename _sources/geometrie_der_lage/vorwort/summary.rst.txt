Summary: Preface (*Vorwort*, Nuremberg, August 1847)
====================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), *Vorwort* (pp. iii–iv)

* **Methodological Autonomy (*Vorwort*)**: :person:`Von Staudt <staudt>` breaks decisively with :person:`Poncelet <poncelet>`, :person:`Möbius <mobius>`, :person:`Steiner <steiner>`, and :person:`Chasles <chasles>` by refusing to prove projective theorems via metrical segment lengths or arithmetic cross-ratios.

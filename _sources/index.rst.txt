GEOMETOR • staudt
=================

.. rubric:: Synthetic Projective Geometry, the Algebra of Throws, & Planar Order Axiomatics — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt (1847, 1857) & Moritz Pasch (1882)

Digital critical edition, synchronized bilingual 4-track Book Codices (*English · Deutsch · Summary · Notes*), exact straightedge-only SymPy constructions, and cross-repository concordance uniting the **Twin Pillars of Modern Projective & Deductive Geometry**:

1. :person:`Karl Georg Christian von Staudt <staudt>`'s *Geometrie der Lage* (:cite:`1847 <staudt:1847>`) and *Beiträge zur Geometrie der Lage* (:cite:`Heft 2, 1857 <staudt:1857>`) — liberating harmonic division, conics, and projective coordinates (:term:`Rechnung mit Würfen <rechnung-mit-wuerfen>`) from metrical length and numerical cross-ratios using **only an unmarked straightedge**.
2. :person:`Moritz Pasch <pasch>`'s *Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie* (:cite:`1882 <pasch:1882>`) — supplying the missing axioms of linear :term:`betweenness <zwischen>` and planar triangle separation (:term:`Pasch's Axiom <pasch-grundsatz>`), reconstructing ideal sheaves without appeal to infinity, and formulating the modern hypothetico-deductive method.

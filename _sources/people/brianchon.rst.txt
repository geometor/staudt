Brianchon
=========

**Charles Julien Brianchon** (*1783–1864*)
   Formulator of Brianchon's Theorem (1806) on hexagons circumscribed about a conic, paired with :person:`Pascal <pascal>`'s Theorem through :term:`projective reciprocity <reciprocitaet>`.

Euclid
======

**Euclid of Alexandria** (*fl. c. 300 BCE*)
   Author of the :cite:`*Elements* <euclid:elements>`, whose tacit reliance on diagram-based linear betweenness and planar crossbar intersections (notably in ``I.16`` and ``I.21``) was first diagnosed and deductively closed by :person:`Moritz Pasch <pasch>`.

Extracting Historical Semantic Knowledge & Repurposing It for Exact Symbolic Reasoning

About This Edition¶

The GEOMETOR edition of Karl Georg Christian von Staudt presents the foundational treatises that resolved synthetic geometry’s nineteenth-century foundational crises and created pure projective geometry without measurement:

  • Karl Georg Christian von Staudt (Geometrie der Lage, 1847; Beiträge zur Geometrie der Lage, 1857): Liberates harmonic division, conics, and projective coordinates (Rechnung mit Würfen) from metrical length and numerical cross-ratios. By constructing the harmonic fourth $D$ of three collinear points purely by unmarked straightedge via the complete plane quadrangle and erecting the synthetic Algebra of Throws, von Staudt proved that projective geometry generates intrinsic numerical coordinates over any commutative field before a single distance is measured or compass opened.

The linear order and planar separation axioms that complete von Staudt’s foundation are unbundled into the standalone sister codex Moritz Pasch: Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie (1882).

This critical edition extracts the implicit deductive architecture of von Staudt’s masterpieces into an addressable, machine-readable semantic corpus. Every proposition, definition, throw operation, and harmonic step is indexed with stable anchors, linked to synchronized four-track codices (English translation, original German print witness, conceptual summary, and scholarly notes), and connected to sister GEOMETOR repositories: classical compass-and-straightedge antecedents in Euclid's Elements, projective pole-polar antecedents in Pappus's Synagoge, axiomatic foundations in Hilbert's Grundlagen, and modern algebraic foundations in geometor.model.

The GEOMETOR Mission¶

GEOMETOR is an ongoing research initiative founded by phi ARCHITECT dedicated to recovering, formalizing, and revitalizing the world’s mathematical and geometric heritage through three foundational pillars:

  1. Extracting Historical Semantic Knowledge: Moving beyond passive page scans and OCR to recover the living deductive structure of 2,300+ years of geometric, axiomatic, and philosophical treatises—elevating prose, marginal citations, primary-source folios (via publish folio), and calibrated Ink-to-Vector .euclid-graphic diagrams into structured, machine-addressable semantic graphs.

  2. Exact Symbolic Grounding (``geometor.model``): Anchoring geometric constructions in 100% exact symbolic algebra (SymPy) and Rational Trigonometry invariants (quadrance, spread, cross-ratio) over exact algebraic fields ($\mathbb{Q}$, $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$) rather than floating-point approximations. In geometor.staudt, every construction is rigorously verified straightedge-only (len(model.circles) == 0, Lemoine simplicity class $S = R$), demonstrating that projective coordinates and harmonic conjugates emerge entirely free from metrical measurement.

  3. Repurposing Knowledge Across Mediums: Re-deploying extracted semantic and algebraic structures across synchronized multi-track Book Codices and EPUB 3 editions (photon-platform/publish), computable deductive canons (geometor.elements, geometor.etc, geometor.pappus, geometor.divine), interactive SVG laboratories, and visual broadcasts across geometor.com.

Sources, Provenance & Methodology¶

The texts and diagrams in this edition derive directly from high-resolution scans of original nineteenth-century German editions preserved in the GEOMETOR archival repository:

  • Staudt (1847): Geometrie der Lage. Nürnberg: Verlag der Friedr. Korn’schen Buchhandlung, 1847 (vi + 216 pp.). Public-domain German witness archived in resources/staudt/sources/staudt_1847_geometrie_der_lage_de.txt.

  • Staudt (1857): Beiträge zur Geometrie der Lage, Zweites Heft (§§ 15–29, arts. 185–408). Nürnberg: Verlag der Friedr. Korn’schen Buchhandlung, 1857. Transcribed in resources/staudt/sources/staudt_1857_beitraege_wuerfe_de.txt.

Scholarly translations and commentaries follow the Digital Scriptorium methodology developed within the GEOMETOR project: strict content/code separation, verbatim transcriptions of primary German witnesses alongside modern English translations, cross-corpus concordance references, and reproducible Python test suites verifying every geometric assertion symbolically.

Beiträge zur Geometrie der Lage (Zweites Heft, 1857) — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt

Author: Karl Georg Christian von Staudt
First German Edition (1857): Beiträge zur Geometrie der Lage, Zweites Heft, Nürnberg: Verlag der Friedr. Korn’schen Buchhandlung, 1857.
English Translation: Original scholarly translation curated for the GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium (2026).
Digital Critical Codex: Synchronized 4-track edition (English · German · Summary · Notes) with calibrated vector figures.

“Four elements of a uniform fundamental form in a definite sequence are called a throw… Through the calculus of throws, the geometry of position becomes completely independent of all metrics.”

—Karl Georg Christian von Staudt, Beiträge zur Geometrie der Lage (1857), Heft 2, § 19

§ 19. The Concept and Addition of Throws (arts. 256–267)

Art. 256 (Concept of a Throw). Four elements $A, B, C, D$ of any uniform fundamental form, taken in a definite order, determine what we call a throw (einen Wurf), written $(ABCD)$. Two throws $(ABCD)$ and $(A'B'C'D')$ situated in the same or in distinct uniform fundamental forms are equal ($(ABCD) = (A'B'C'D')$) when and only when the two forms can be projectively related so that $A, B, C, D$ correspond in order to $A', B', C', D'$.

§ 29. The Harmonic Scale & Projective Abscissae (arts. 403–404)

Art. 403. Repeated addition of the unit element $P_1$ produces on $\ell$ the harmonic scale $P_1, P_2, P_3, \dots, P_n, \dots$, where each consecutive triad $(P_{n-1}, P_n, P_{n+1})$ forms with $P_\infty$ a harmonic tetrad $(P_{n-1}, P_{n+1}; P_n, P_\infty) = -1$. Taking additive inverses yields $P_{-1}, P_{-2}, \dots$, and straightedge division ($P_p \cdot P_q^{-1}$) assigns a unique point $P_{p/q}$ on $\ell$ to every positive and negative rational number $p/q \in \mathbb{Q}$.

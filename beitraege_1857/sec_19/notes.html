Critical Notes: § 19. The Concept and Addition of Throws (arts. 256–267)¶

Scholarly Commentary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — Beiträge, Zweites Heft (1857), § 19 (arts. 256–267)

  • Desargues Gives a Division Ring; Pappus Gives Commutativity: Modern projective algebra (Hilbert 1899 Kap. V–VI, Hessenberg 1905, Veblen & Young 1910 Ch. VI) revealed the exact axiomatic anatomy inside von Staudt’s 1857 constructions:

    • Desargues’s Theorem alone suffices to prove that construct_von_staudt_addition and construct_von_staudt_multiplication are independent of \((U, V)\), that \((\ell \setminus \{P_\infty\}, +)\) is an abelian group (even over a non-commutative skew-field!), and that \((\ell \setminus \{P_0, P_\infty\}, \cdot)\) is an associative group distributing over \(+\), making \(\ell \setminus \{P_\infty\}\) a division ring (skew-field).

    • Pappus’s Hexagon Theorem (geometor/pappus VII.139) is necessary and sufficient for projective multiplication to be commutative (\(X \cdot Y = Y \cdot X\))! And by Gerhard Hessenberg’s theorem (1905), Pappus’s theorem in a projective plane automatically implies Desargues’s theorem.

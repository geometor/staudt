Critical Notes: § 20. Pure Straightedge Multiplication of Throws (arts. 268–269)
================================================================================

.. rubric:: Scholarly Commentary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Beiträge, Zweites Heft* (1857), § 20 (arts. 268–269)

* **Pappus Hexagon & Field Multiplication**: Modern projective geometry proved that while Desargues's Theorem guarantees associativity and distributivity of multiplication, **Pappus's Hexagon Theorem** (``geometor/pappus`` ``VII.139``) is **necessary and sufficient** for multiplication to be **commutative** (:math:`X \cdot Y = Y \cdot X`)!

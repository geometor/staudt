Summary: § 20. Pure Straightedge Multiplication of Throws (arts. 268–269)
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Beiträge, Zweites Heft* (1857), § 20 (arts. 268–269)

* **Straightedge Multiplication (§ 20 art. 268)**: Centers $U, V$ collinear with $P_0$ and axis $a = [P_\infty W]$ ($W = [U P_1] \cap [V X]$) construct $P = X \cdot Y$ by a hyperbolic double perspectivity fixing $P_0$ and $P_\infty$.

Summary: § 20. Pure Straightedge Multiplication of Throws (arts. 268–269)
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Beiträge, Zweites Heft* (1857), § 20 (arts. 268–269)

* **Straightedge Multiplication (§ 20 art. 268)**: Centers :math:`U, V` collinear with :math:`P_0` and axis :math:`a = [P_\infty W]` (:math:`W = [U P_1] \cap [V X]`) construct :math:`P = X \cdot Y` by a hyperbolic double perspectivity fixing :math:`P_0` and :math:`P_\infty`.

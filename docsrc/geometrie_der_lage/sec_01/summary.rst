Summary: § 1. Space, Surfaces, Lines, and Points (arts. 1–15)
=============================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 1 (arts. 1–15)

* **Spatial Incidence (§ 1)**: Straight lines and planes are characterized by incidence determination ($A, B \mapsto [AB]$; non-collinear $A, B, C \mapsto \text{plane } ABC$; two meeting planes share a whole line).

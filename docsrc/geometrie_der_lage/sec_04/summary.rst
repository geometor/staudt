Summary: § 4. Cyclical Order and Mutual Separation (arts. 36–44)
================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 4 (arts. 36–44)

* **Cyclical Order and Separation (§ 4)**: On the closed projective line, separation requires **four** points (:math:`(A, B)` separating :math:`(C, D)`), replacing linear betweenness with topological four-point cyclic separation.

Critical Notes: § 5. Uniform Fundamental Forms and Perspectivity (arts. 45–53)
==============================================================================

.. rubric:: Scholarly Commentary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 5 (arts. 45–53)

* **The Three 1D Carriers**: By isolating the point-row, ray-pencil, and plane-pencil as structurally isomorphic carriers of one-dimensional projective geometry, von Staudt prepares the ground for the pure incidence definition of projectivity without coordinates or metrical cross-ratios.

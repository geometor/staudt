Summary: § 6. Complete Plane Quadrangles and Harmonic Forms (arts. 66–74)
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 6 (arts. 66–74)

* **Complete Plane Quadrangle (§ 6, art. 66)**: Four general coplanar points :math:`E, F, G, H` determine six sides in three opposite pairs meeting at three diagonal points; plane-dual to the complete quadrilateral.
* **Metric-Free Harmonic Fourth (§ 6, art. 67)**: Four collinear points :math:`(A, B; C, D)` are :term:`harmonic <harmonisches-gebilde>` iff two opposite quadrangle sides meet at :math:`A`, two meet at :math:`B`, and the remaining two cut :math:`[AB]` at :math:`C` and :math:`D`—requiring **7 straightedge lines and 0 circles**.
* **3D Incidence Proof of Uniqueness (§ 6, art. 70)**: Lifting to an auxiliary plane :math:`\alpha'` through :math:`\ell` proves purely from spatial plane intersections (equivalent in the plane to :person:`Desargues <desargues>`'s Two-Triangle Theorem) that :math:`D` is independent of the quadrangle.

Summary: § 6. Complete Plane Quadrangles and Harmonic Forms (arts. 66–74)
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 6 (arts. 66–74)

* **Complete Plane Quadrangle (§ 6, art. 66)**: Four general coplanar points $E, F, G, H$ determine six sides in three opposite pairs meeting at three diagonal points; plane-dual to the complete quadrilateral.
* **Metric-Free Harmonic Fourth (§ 6, art. 67)**: Four collinear points $(A, B; C, D)$ are :term:`harmonic <harmonisches-gebilde>` iff two opposite quadrangle sides meet at $A$, two meet at $B$, and the remaining two cut $[AB]$ at $C$ and $D$—requiring **7 straightedge lines and 0 circles**.
* **3D Incidence Proof of Uniqueness (§ 6, art. 70)**: Lifting to an auxiliary plane $\alpha'$ through $\ell$ proves purely from spatial plane intersections (equivalent in the plane to :person:`Desargues <desargues>`'s Two-Triangle Theorem) that $D$ is independent of the quadrangle.

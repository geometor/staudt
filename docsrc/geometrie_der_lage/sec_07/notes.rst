Critical Notes: § 7. Perspective Projection of Harmonic Tetrads (arts. 75–84)
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Scholarly Commentary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 7 (arts. 75–84)

* **Harmonic Invariance Across Projections**: Von Staudt shows that a central projection between two 1D carriers carries a complete quadrangle into a complete quadrangle, proving that the harmonic relation is an invariant of perspectivity prior to defining general projectivities.

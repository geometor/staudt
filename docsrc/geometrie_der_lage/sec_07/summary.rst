Summary: § 7. Perspective Projection of Harmonic Tetrads (arts. 75–84)
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 7 (arts. 75–84)

* **Harmonic Perspectivity (§ 7, art. 76)**: Every perspective projection between uniform fundamental forms preserves harmonic tetrads, establishing harmonic invariance under central projection.

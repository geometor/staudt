Summary: § 8. Pure Synthetic Projectivity & The Fundamental Theorem (arts. 85–98)
=================================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 8 (arts. 85–98)

* **Harmonic Definition of Projectivity (§ 8, art. 93)**: Replaces :person:`Steiner <steiner>`'s and :person:`Chasles <chasles>`'s cross-ratio definition with harmonic-tetrad preservation.
* **Fundamental Theorem (§ 8, art. 96)**: Three distinct elements $(A, B, C) \mapsto (A', B', C')$ uniquely determine a projectivity on a uniform fundamental form.

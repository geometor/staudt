Critical Notes: § 9. Involutions in Uniform Fundamental Forms (arts. 103–115)
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Scholarly Commentary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 9 (arts. 103–115)

* **Bridge to *Beiträge* Heft 1 (1856) — Synthetic Imaginary Points**: Notice how § 9 (Art. 109) and § 19 (Art. 245) prepare von Staudt's next triumph (:cite:`Beiträge, Heft 1, 1856 <staudt:1856>`): when an exterior line $g$ fails to meet $\mathcal{C}$ in real points, $\pi$ **still** induces a completely well-defined real elliptic involution on $g$! Equipping that elliptic involution with one of its two opposite cyclical senses $(A B A' B')$ vs. $(A B' A' B)$ gave geometry its first rigorous, purely real synthetic model of **conjugate complex intersections** without $\sqrt{-1}$.

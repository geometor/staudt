Summary: § 18. Planar Polar Systems (arts. 235–241)
===================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 18 (arts. 235–241)

* **Planar Polar System (§ 18, arts. 235–238)**: An involutory point-line correlation :math:`\pi` (:math:`Q \in \pi(P) \iff P \in \pi(Q)`) is determined by a self-polar triangle plus one generic pole–polar pair.

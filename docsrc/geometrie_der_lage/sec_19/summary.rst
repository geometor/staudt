Summary: § 19. Curves of the Second Order & Polar Generation (arts. 242–257)
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Summary — Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — *Geometrie der Lage* (1847), § 19 (arts. 242–257)

* **Intrinsic Conic Definition (§ 19, arts. 242–245)**: Defines an :term:`order curve <ordnungscurve>` (conic) purely in the plane as :math:`\{X : X \in \pi(X)\}`, instantly unifying tangents, harmonic pole-polar secants, :person:`Steiner <steiner>`'s pencil generation, and :person:`Pascal <pascal>`–:person:`Brianchon <brianchon>` duality.

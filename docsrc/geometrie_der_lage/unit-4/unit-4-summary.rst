Unit 4 Summary: Coordinates Without Measure (The Algebra of Throws)
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Synopsis of *Beiträge zur Geometrie der Lage, Heft 2* (1857), §§ 19, 20, 29

1. **Throw (*Wurf*, § 19 art. 256)**: An ordered projective equivalence class $(ABCD)$ of four collinear elements; fixing gauge triad $(P_0, P_1, P_\infty)$ identifies each point $X \in \ell \setminus \{P_\infty\}$ with $(P_\infty P_0 P_1 X)$.
2. **Straightedge Addition (§ 19 art. 259)**: Centers $U, V$ collinear with $P_\infty$ and axis $a = [P_\infty R]$ ($R = [U P_0] \cap [V X]$) construct $S = X + Y$ by a parabolic double perspectivity fixing $P_\infty$.
3. **Straightedge Multiplication (§ 20 art. 268)**: Centers $U, V$ collinear with $P_0$ and axis $a = [P_\infty W]$ ($W = [U P_1] \cap [V X]$) construct $P = X \cdot Y$ by a hyperbolic double perspectivity fixing $P_0$ and $P_\infty$.
4. **Harmonic Scale & Projective Abscissae (§ 29 arts. 403–404)**: Iterated sums and quotients construct every rational coordinate $P_{p/q}$ by straightedge alone, coordinatizing $\mathbb{P}^1$ intrinsically without metric distance.

Unit 1 Summary: Bounded Segments & Linear Betweenness Axioms
============================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Synopsis of *Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie* (1882), *Vorwort*, *Einleitung* & § 1

1. **Bounded Segments Before Infinite Lines (*Einleitung*)**: :person:`Pasch <pasch>` begins from finite observable **straight segments** $AB$ and the ternary relation "$C$ lies inside $AB$" (:term:`betweenness <zwischen>`) rather than assuming completed infinite lines *a priori*.
2. **Eight Nuclear Linear Axioms (§ 1, *Grundsätze* I–VIII)**: Axiomatize unique segment connection (I), interior density (II), asymmetry (III), convex inclusion (IV), two-part partition around an interior point (V), arbitrarily repeatable prolongation (VI–VII), and unique two-point line determination (VIII).
3. **Derived Line, Opposite Rays & Ordered Quadruples (*Lehrsätze* 1–23)**: Constructs the full line $[AB]$, proves two-ray division by any single point $O$ (*Lehrsatz* 12, ruling out closed spherical/elliptic circles!), and proves canonical ordering $A\text{--}B\text{--}C\text{--}D$ of any four collinear points (*Lehrsatz* 19).

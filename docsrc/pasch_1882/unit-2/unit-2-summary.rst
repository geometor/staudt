Unit 2 Summary: Pasch's Axiom, Finite Sheaves & Formal Deduction
================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Synopsis of *Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie* (1882), §§ 2, 8–12

1. **Pasch's Planar Separation Axiom (§ 2, *IV. Grundsatz*, p. 21)**: Any coplanar line avoiding vertices $A, B, C$ that enters open side $AB$ must exit through open side $AC$ or open side $BC$.
2. **Exclusive Exit & Plane Separation (*10. Lehrsatz*, p. 25)**: Applying *Grundsatz IV* to sub-triangle $\triangle BDE$ proves no line can cut all three open sides of $\triangle ABC$, partitioning $\alpha \setminus a$ into two convex half-planes.
3. **Ideal Sheaves Without Infinity (§§ 8–11)**: Replaces Euclidean "points at infinity" by spatial ray-sheaves (*Strahlenbündel*) constructed from finite plane intersections, and proves harmonic separation deductively via *Grundsatz IV*.
4. **The Hypothetico-Deductive Manifesto (§ 12, p. 98)**: Inference must be completely independent of conceptual meanings and diagrams; any need to consult intuition exposes a missing axiom, while formal completeness makes projective duality automatic.

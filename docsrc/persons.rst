People
======

.. rubric:: Geometers, Algebraists & Philosophers of the Projective and Axiomatic Tradition

Authors & Primary Pioneers
--------------------------

.. _person-staudt:

**Karl Georg Christian von Staudt** (*1798–1867*)
   Professor of Mathematics at the University of Erlangen (1835–1867); student of Carl Friedrich Gauss at Göttingen. In *Geometrie der Lage* (:cite:`1847 <staudt:1847>`) and *Beiträge zur Geometrie der Lage* (:cite:`1856–1860 <staudt:1857>`), he emancipated projective geometry from metrical length and numerical cross-ratio, defining harmonic tetrads, projectivities, conics, imaginary elements, and projective coordinates (:term:`Rechnung mit Würfen <rechnung-mit-wuerfen>`) purely through incidence and separation.

.. _person-pasch:

**Moritz Pasch** (*1843–1930*)
   Professor of Mathematics at the University of Giessen. In *Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie* (:cite:`1882 <pasch:1882>`), he uncovered the unstated order hypotheses of classical and projective geometry, axiomatized linear :term:`betweenness <zwischen>` and planar triangle separation (:term:`Pasch's Axiom <pasch-grundsatz>`), constructed ideal projective sheaves intrinsically from proper segments and planes, and articulated the modern hypothetico-deductive criterion of formal proof (§ 12, p. 98).

Cited Geometers, Philosophers & Scholars
----------------------------------------

.. _person-euclid:

**Euclid of Alexandria** (*fl. c. 300 BCE*)
   Author of the :cite:`*Elements* <euclid:elements>`, whose tacit reliance on diagram-based linear betweenness and planar crossbar intersections (notably in ``I.16`` and ``I.21``) was first diagnosed and deductively closed by :person:`Moritz Pasch <pasch>`.

.. _person-pappus:

**Pappus of Alexandria** (*c. 290–c. 350 CE*)
   Author of the :cite:`*Synagoge* <pappus:synagoge>`, Book VII of which preserved the invariance of collinear four-point cross-ratios and the Hexagon Theorem (``VII.139``), which in :person:`von Staudt <staudt>`'s Algebra of Throws becomes the geometric law of multiplicative commutativity ($X \cdot Y = Y \cdot X$).

.. _person-desargues:

**Girard Desargues** (*1591–1661*)
   French architect and projective pioneer (*Brouillon project*, 1639), whose Two-Triangle Theorem guarantees that :person:`von Staudt <staudt>`'s complete-quadrangle harmonic fourth depends solely on the three collinear base points and that projective addition and multiplication form a division ring.

.. _person-pascal:

**Blaise Pascal** (*1623–1662*)
   Discoverer of the *Hexagrammum Mysticum* (1640) on hexagons inscribed in a conic, deduced synthetically without coordinates in :person:`von Staudt <staudt>`'s theory of planar polar systems and :term:`order curves <ordnungscurve>` (1847 § 19).

.. _person-brianchon:

**Charles Julien Brianchon** (*1783–1864*)
   Formulator of Brianchon's Theorem (1806) on hexagons circumscribed about a conic, paired with :person:`Pascal <pascal>`'s Theorem through :term:`projective reciprocity <reciprocitaet>`.

.. _person-poncelet:

**Jean-Victor Poncelet** (*1788–1867*)
   Author of the *Traité des propriétés projectives des figures* (1822), who revived synthetic projective geometry, ideal elements, and pole–polar duality.

.. _person-mobius:

**August Ferdinand Möbius** (*1790–1868*)
   Author of :cite:`*Der barycentrische Calcul* (1827) <mobius:1827>`, originator of barycentric coordinates and the rational projective net (*Möbiussches Netz*) generated by iterated quadrangle constructions.

.. _person-steiner:

**Jakob Steiner** (*1796–1863*)
   Swiss geometer at Berlin (:cite:`1832 <steiner:1832>`, :cite:`1833 <steiner:1833>`) who generated conics from projective pencils and proved straightedge-only parallel constructions, yet retained metrical anharmonic ratios where :person:`von Staudt <staudt>` demanded pure incidence.

.. _person-chasles:

**Michel Chasles** (*1793–1880*)
   French geometer who codified the signed metrical cross-ratio (*rapport anharmonique*) and homographic correspondences.

.. _person-klein:

**Felix Klein** (*1849–1925*)
   Author of the Erlangen Program and two landmark memoirs on non-Euclidean geometry (:cite:`1871 <klein:1871>`, :cite:`1873 <klein:1873>`), who grounded Cayley's projective metrics on :person:`von Staudt <staudt>`'s coordinates while pointing out the continuity gap later addressed by :person:`Darboux <darboux>` and :person:`Pasch <pasch>`.

.. _person-darboux:

**Jean-Gaston Darboux** (*1842–1917*)
   French analyst and geometer who proved (:cite:`1880 <darboux:1880>`) that over $\mathbb{R}$ every harmonic-preserving bijection is continuous and projective, vindicating von Staudt's Fundamental Theorem over the real continuum.

.. _person-hilbert:

**David Hilbert** (*1862–1943*)
   Author of :cite:`*Grundlagen der Geometrie* (1899) <hilbert:1899>`, who adopted :person:`Pasch <pasch>`'s planar separation postulate as Axiom II 4 and adapted :person:`von Staudt <staudt>`'s Algebra of Throws into his Chapter III *Streckenrechnung*.

.. _person-russell:

**Bertrand Russell** (*1872–1970*)
   British philosopher and logician whose :cite:`*Essay on the Foundations of Geometry* (1897) <russell:1897>` and :cite:`*Principles of Mathematics* (1903) <russell:1903>` critically examined von Staudt's quadrangle construction and Pasch's descriptive order axioms.

.. _person-vailati:

**Giovanni Vailati** (*1863–1909*)
   Italian logician of the Peano school (:cite:`1895 <vailati:1895>`) who axiomatized cyclical separation of point pairs on closed projective lines.

.. _person-veblen:

**Oswald Veblen** (*1880–1960*)
   American geometer who with J. W. Young (:cite:`1910 <veblen:1910>`) recast von Staudt's Algebra of Throws as the modern *Algebra of Points* over arbitrary division rings and fields.

.. _person-hoenen:

**Peter Hubert Jacob Hoenen, S.J.** (*1880–1961*)
   Author of :cite:`*De Noetica Geometriae* (1954) <hoenen:1954>`, analyzing Pasch's :term:`Stammsätze <stammsaetze>` and formalist deduction from the standpoint of Aristotelian-Thomistic epistemology.

.. _person-tarski:

**Alfred Tarski** (*1901–1983*)
   Polish-American logician (:cite:`1959 <tarski:1959>`) whose first-order elementary geometry distills planar order into the inner/outer five-point Pasch axiom.

Audit and verify Pasch’s IV. Grundsatz (1882, § 2, p. 21) & 10. Lehrsatz (p. 25) on \(\triangle ABC\).

Given non-collinear vertices \(A, B, C\) in the plane and a straight line \(\ell = [P Q]\):

  • Computes exact signed half-plane orientations \(\operatorname{orient}(P, Q, V) \in \{-1, 0, +1\}\) for each vertex \(V \in \{A, B, C\}\).

  • When \(\ell\) passes through no vertex (\(\operatorname{orient} \neq 0\) for all three): if \(\ell\) meets any open triangle side strictly between its endpoints (is_between()), it must exit strictly through one and only one of the remaining two sides (10. Lehrsatz, p. 25: it cannot meet all three open sides, nor meet only one).

geometor.staudt.pasch.demonstrate_euclid_i16_pasch_gap() → tuple[geometor.model.Model, dict[str, Any]]¶

Construct Euclid I.16 (Exterior Angle Theorem) and verify the Pasch order step.

In Elements Book I Prop. 16, Euclid bisects \(AC\) at \(E\), prolongs median \(BE\) to \(F\) with \(BE = EF\), joins \(CF\), and infers from visual inspection of the diagram that \(F\) lies inside exterior angle \(\angle ACD\) so that \(\angle ACD > \angle ECF\) (Common Notion 5). On a sphere (elliptic plane) where straight lines are closed geodesics lacking open linear betweenness, \(F\) can wrap past the antipode and violate I.16.

Using Pasch’s linear betweenness (§ 1) and planar separation (IV. Grundsatz, § 2): since \(C\) lies between \(B\) and \(D\) (\(B\text{--}C\text{--}D\)), \(E\) lies between \(A\) and \(C\) (\(A\text{--}E\text{--}C\)), and \(E\) lies between \(B\) and \(F\) (\(B\text{--}E\text{--}F\)), ray \(CF\) lies strictly inside \(\angle ACD\) (i.e., any segment joining \(A_1 \in CA_{\text{ext}}\) or \(D \in BC_{\text{ext}}\) to \(A\) is cut internally by ray \(CF\) via Pasch’s Axiom on \(\triangle ABD\)).

geometor.staudt.pasch.demonstrate_euclid_i21_pasch_gap() → tuple[geometor.model.Model, dict[str, Any]]¶

Prove Euclid I.21’s unstated ray-exit assumption directly from Pasch’s IV. Grundsatz.

In Elements Book I Prop. 21, given \(\triangle ABC\) and an interior point \(D\), Euclid writes “let BD be drawn through to E [on AC]” without proving that prolonging \(BD\) must intersect open side \(AC\) between \(A\) and \(C\).

Deductive proof via Pasch’s Axiom (Vorlesungen § 2, p. 21):

  1. For interior point \(D\) of \(\triangle ABC\), line \([CD]\) meets open side \(AB\) at \(M\) strictly between \(A\) and \(B\), with \(D\) strictly between \(C\) and \(M\).

  2. Apply demonstrate_pasch_axiom() to sub-triangle \(\triangle AMC\) and transversal \(\ell = [BD]\):

    • \(\ell\) misses all three vertices \(A, M, C\) (since \(A\text{--}M\text{--}B\)).

    • \(\ell\) enters side \(CM\) strictly at \(D\).

    • \(\ell\) cannot exit side \(AM\), because \(\ell \cap [AM] = B\) lies outside segment \(AM\).

    • Therefore, by Pasch’s Grundsatz IV, \(\ell = [BD]\) must exit side \(AC\) at an interior point \(E\) strictly between \(A\) and \(C\)!
